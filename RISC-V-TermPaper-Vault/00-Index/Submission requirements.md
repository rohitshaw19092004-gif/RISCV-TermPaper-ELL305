## Strengthening of the model study (15 November 2026)

Three additions, made before the Turnitin resubmission because they change
tables and source digests.

- Timing tests: 20 new ones (35 in all) in `run_study.py`. Each compares the
  simulator with a value derived by hand from the rules of Appendices N and O:
  the page walk (16 against 4 cycles), the committed-path rule of every defense
  (Taint and NoFwd 25, NoSpecLoad 29, DoM 15 on a missing chain, no change for
  DoM on hits and for the invisible schemes), the delayed redirect of Undo (42
  and 56 against 28), the commit floor of Invis-V (3 cycles) and a context
  switch with a flush (322, 332, 334). All 35 match exactly. Loop counts in a
  differential test must give `li` the same length (for example 3000 and 6000),
  otherwise the code shifts by one instruction and an extra I-cache line costs
  216 cycles once.
- Combined defenses (E7) now run over five page-frame layouts (500 runs, 2651 in
  all). Table 10.8 gives means and the range of the excess. All eight
  combinations that deviate from the product by more than one point keep their
  sign in every layout, so the deviations are not layout noise; 17 of 25 are
  within one point of the product and 20 of 25 within one point of the sum.
- Appendix M states the race arithmetic: an epoch allows 100 last-level
  accesses per candidate; a quadratic search needs about 330 epochs, group
  testing about 272 accesses per candidate, a little under three epochs.

Still not done, by choice: no comparison of the timing with gem5 or RTL, and no
attack program (Section 1.6 excludes one).
