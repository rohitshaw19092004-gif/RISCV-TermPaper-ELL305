Length target: 150 to 200 pages. Single author.

## Still to do by hand

- [ ] Submit the compiled PDF to Turnitin.
- [ ] Copy the figures of the Turnitin report into the `\Turnitin...` macros near
      the top of the `.tex` (date, submission id, overall similarity, internet
      sources, publications, student papers, largest source) and recompile.
      Appendix A prints them. The report in the paper must match the Turnitin
      submission.
- [ ] Show the paper to a TA or lab staff member before submission; add their
      name to the Acknowledgements if they helped.
- [ ] Upload the `.tex` and the PDF to Gradescope.
- [ ] Commit, tag and push the repository so that the link on the title page
      shows the final version.
